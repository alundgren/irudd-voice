# Vocabulary file

`~/Library/Application Support/irudd-voice/vocabulary.json`, written by
`irudd-voice vocab`, read by the app at model load and by training.

```json
{"version": 1, "generated_at": "2026-10-25T10:00:00Z", "terms": ["T3 Code", "SwiftUI", "irudd-voice"]}
```

Empty (`"terms": []`) or missing means no vocabulary. Terms are kept in the
spelling to use. At most 200 terms go into the prompt, most frequent first.
